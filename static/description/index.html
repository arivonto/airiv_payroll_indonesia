AIRIV Indonesia Payroll Operations

Indonesia Payroll PPh 21 TER & BPJS Calculation Engine

Payroll calculation workspace for Odoo 18 with Indonesian PPh 21 TER, gross-up support, BPJS employment and health contributions, payslip review, and auditable payroll preparation.

Odoo 18 CommunityPPh 21 TERBPJSPayrollAIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV Payroll Indonesia brings Indonesian tax and contribution context into a structured payroll review workflow connected to employee and contract data.

PPh 21 TER

Indonesian tax calculation

Prepare monthly employee tax context using TER categories, annualization, and reviewable calculation results.

BPJS

Employment and health

Calculate and review BPJS employment and health contribution context for payroll operations.

Payslip Review

Gross to net visibility

Keep gross salary, deductions, tax, contributions, net pay, and approval status visible for review.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Configure rules

Set tax categories, rates, BPJS parameters, salary structures, and company policy.

2

Prepare payroll

Select period, employees, contracts, attendance, and salary inputs.

3

Calculate

Run gross, deductions, PPh 21 TER, BPJS, and net pay calculations.

4

Review and approve

Inspect exceptions, approve payslips, and retain payroll records for audit.

Installation Guidance

Install

Add to Odoo addons

Clone branch 18.0, place the module on the addons path, restart Odoo, update the Apps list, and install.

Configure

Set payroll rules

Configure PPh 21 TER, BPJS, salary structures, company parameters, and payroll access groups.

Operate

Run payroll review

Prepare a period, calculate payslips, inspect exceptions, approve, and retain records.
